Guidely Expense Policy

Employees can claim business-related expenses such as meals, travel, and accommodation.
Meals during travel are reimbursable up to $50 per day. Receipt uploads are mandatory for all claims exceeding $25.
Flight tickets must be booked in economy class. Business class is permitted only for international flights exceeding 8 hours.
Hotel stays must not exceed $150 per night, unless staying in high-cost cities like New York or Tokyo where the limit is $250.
Submit all expense reports through the HR Portal by the 5th of the following month. Delayed submissions will be rejected.
