# Guidely Corporate Policies and Regulations

## 1. Remote Work Policy
Guidely operates under a flexible, hybrid work model. All employees are allowed to work remotely up to 100% of the time, provided they maintain communication during core hours (**10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**) and achieve their deliverables. The company provides a remote work allowance of **$500 USD** once per year to purchase home office equipment (ergonomic chairs, monitors, keyboard).

## 2. Travel Expense Policy
All corporate travel must be pre-approved by your team manager. Reimbursable categories include:
- **Accommodation**: Standard hotel rooms up to **$200 USD per night**.
- **Meals**: Daily food and beverage reimbursement is capped at **$75 USD**.
- **Transport**: Standard class flights, trains, and rideshares are fully covered.
- **Receipts**: Itemized receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $25 USD. Credit card statements alone are not accepted.

## 3. Equipment and Asset Policy
All laptops, testing mobile phones, and security keys provided by Guidely remain company property.
- **Security Patches**: You must install system security updates within 48 hours of release.
- **Loss or Theft**: Report lost or stolen equipment to `security@guidely.com` immediately.
- **Return of Assets**: Upon resignation or termination, all hardware must be returned to HR within 5 business days.

## 4. Code Review and Deployments
We enforce a strict code quality policy to avoid visual layout bugs and broken APIs:
- All pull requests require at least one approving review from a senior engineer.
- Unit tests must be run and pass locally before making a PR.
- Tailwind CSS v4 variables must be structured inside `index.css` under the `@theme` directive.
- Direct pushes to the `main` branch are blocked.
