# GUIDELY INC. OFFICIAL CORPORATE POLICIES

## Section 1: Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Guidely Inc. supports a modern, hybrid working environment that balances individual schedule flexibility with team alignment. All employees are eligible to work remotely up to 100% of the time, subject to manager approval and meeting quarterly deliverables.
- **Core Hours Availability**: While working remotely, employees must remain online and responsive on corporate communication channels (Slack and Gmail) during core hours, defined as **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST** daily.
- **Home Office Allowance**: To support ergonomic, safe home workspaces, Guidely provides a annual workspace allowance of **$500 USD** for purchasing office furniture, monitors, or keyboards. Submit purchase receipts through the expense portal.
- **Utility Reimbursement**: Employees are eligible for a monthly stipend of $50 USD to assist with high-speed home internet and electricity expenses.

## Section 2: Expense Policy
All travel on company business must be pre-approved by your department head and align with our client project budgets. Reimbursable expense caps are enforced as follows:
- **Accommodation Caps**: Lodging is capped at a maximum of **$200 USD per night**, excluding taxes and local service fees. Exceptions require executive director approval.
- **Meal Reimbursements**: The daily cap for meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and beverages) is **$75 USD**.
- **Transportation**: Standard class flights, trains, and rideshares are fully covered. Car rentals must be pre-approved and limited to mid-size vehicles.
- **Expense Reporting**: All expense claims must be accompanied by itemized receipt files and submitted within 30 days of the travel date. Credit card slips are not sufficient.

## Section 3: Data Protection and Client Confidentiality Policy
Protecting our clients' proprietary information, business strategies, and operational data is critical to our corporate success:
- **Confidential Information**: Never share client names, logos, financials, or meeting transcripts on public forums, personal social media, or unapproved communication channels.
- **Secure Networking**: Employees must activate the secure corporate VPN whenever accessing internal files or database records from public networks (such as coffee shops or airports).
- **Password Integrity**: Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all corporate systems. Passwords must be updated every 90 days.
- **Clean Desk Policy**: Ensure no sensitive documents or security badges are left visible on your desk when stepping away. Lock your workstation screens.

## Section 4: Professional Code of Conduct and Inclusion
Guidely is dedicated to providing an inclusive, safe, and collaborative work environment:
- **Mutual Respect**: We hold our team members to high standards of professional courtesy during client meetings, team alignments, and online discussions.
- **Anti-Harassment**: Guidely enforces a zero-tolerance policy for harassment or discrimination of any kind based on race, gender, religion, age, or disability.
- **Reporting Violations**: Report any code of conduct violations directly to HR or use the anonymous corporate reporting portal. HR reviews all logs and conducts audits.
