# Guidely Corporate Policies and Employee Code of Conduct

## 1. Hybrid and Remote Work Policy
Guidely supports a hybrid work environment. Employees have the flexibility to work from home up to 100% of the time, provided they are available during core business hours (**10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**) and hit their team targets. To support comfortable home setups, Guidely provides a home office allowance of **$500 USD** once per year for purchasing ergonomic furniture, external monitors, or office accessories.

## 2. Business Travel and Expense Policy
Travel on company business must be approved by your department head. Covered expenses include:
- **Hotels**: Lodging reimbursement is capped at **$200 USD per night**.
- **Meals**: Food and beverage expense is capped at **$75 USD per day**.
- **Receipts**: Itemized receipts are mandatory for all expense claims. Credit card slips are not sufficient.
- **Reporting**: Expense reports must be submitted through the portal within 30 days of returning from travel.

## 3. Data Confidentiality and Client Privacy
As a consulting firm, protecting client information is our top priority.
- Never share client names, logos, or operational data on public forums or unapproved chat systems.
- Use corporate VPNs when working from public Wi-Fi networks.
- Lock your workstation screens whenever you step away from your desk.

## 4. Professional Code of Conduct
Guidely is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free workplace for everyone. We hold our team members to high standards of mutual respect, professional speech, and ethical behavior during client meetings and internal discussions. Violations are audited by HR and may lead to disciplinary action.
