# Guidely Employee Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Q: When is the monthly pay schedule?
A: Salaries are processed and deposited directly into your bank account on the **25th of every month**. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, payments are made on the preceding business day.

## Q: How do I request vacation time?
A: Annual leave requests must be submitted through our HR Portal at least two weeks in advance. Once your manager approves the request, the days will be deducted from your yearly vacation balance.

## Q: What is the daily expense reimbursement cap?
A: For approved business travel, the daily reimbursement cap for meals is **$75 USD**. Itemized receipts are required for all transactions above $25 USD.

## Q: Where do I get office parking access?
A: Parking passes are managed by the Facilities Team. New employees can request a monthly parking sticker during their first week. Spots are available on a first-come, first-served basis in the basement garage.

## Q: What software tools are approved for client communications?
A: Guidely approved tools for client engagements are Zoom, Slack, and email. Any other communication tool must receive approval from the IT Security Team prior to client contact.

## Q: How do I access the office outside business hours?
A: Your corporate badge is configured for 24/7 access to all regional hubs. If you experience badge errors, contact the Security desk or email `security@guidely.com`.
