Guidely Remote Work FAQ

Q: What are the core hours?
A: Our core hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM EST. All employees are expected to be available for collaboration during this period.

Q: Can I work from another country?
A: You can work remotely from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. You must inform HR and obtain manager approval.

Q: Is there an internet stipend?
A: Yes, remote employees receive a monthly stipend of $50 for home internet bills. Submit your invoice through the expense portal.

Q: How do I request equipment?
A: Submit a ticket in the IT Helpdesk under 'Hardware Request'. Standard equipment includes a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse.
