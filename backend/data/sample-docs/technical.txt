# Guidely Corporate Operations Technical Guidelines

This document outlines the standard operating procedures and technical resources utilized by Guidely employees in client service and corporate administration roles.

## 1. Client Database and CRM (Salesforce)
We use Salesforce to manage our customer relations, advisory leads, and historical contract data.
- **Data Input**: Operations staff must log every client call and project status change within 24 hours.
- **Reporting**: Weekly sales funnel reports are compiled automatically on Friday afternoons.
- **Access Control**: Logins are secured via single sign-on (SSO) and require multi-factor authentication (MFA).

## 2. File Organization and Cloud Storage
Guidely utilizes secure corporate Google Drive folders to store project deliverable assets:
- **Directory Naming**: Always use the format `[Client Name] - [Project Name] - [Year]` for root client directories.
- **Sensitive Folders**: Place client financials and salary grids inside subfolders named `Confidential_HR`.
- **Retention**: Active project assets are archived to offline servers 12 months after project completion.

## 3. IT Security and Workstation Protection
To comply with global data safety regulations, all corporate laptops must follow these protocols:
- **Operating System Updates**: Ensure security patches are installed within 48 hours of prompt.
- **Password Complexity**: Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
- **Corporate VPN**: The Guidely secure VPN must be active whenever accessing internal files from home or public networks.

## 4. Software Tool Stack for Collaboration
Guidely provides licenses for the following approved communication and productivity utilities:
- **Slack**: For instant messaging, department threads, and corporate announcements.
- **Zoom**: For client presentations, sales pitches, and regional team meetings.
- **Google Workspace**: For shared documents, project spreadsheets, and corporate slide decks.
- **Asana**: For project checklist coordination and deliverable tracking.
