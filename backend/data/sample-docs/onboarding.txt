# Guidely Corporate HR Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to Guidely Inc.!
We are thrilled to welcome you to our growing team. This guide outlines your onboarding roadmap, benefit enrollments, and first-week priorities.

## First Week Checklist
- **Day 1**: Attend the HR orientation session, complete tax and payroll documentation, and receive your corporate ID card.
- **Day 2**: Meet your team lead, set up your email and communication accounts, and join the company welcome thread.
- **Day 3**: Review the Guidely Corporate Profile and complete the operational onboarding module.
- **Day 4**: Review your quarterly deliverables and schedule your first 1-on-1 alignment check-in.
- **Day 5**: Complete your benefits enrollment form (health, dental, and retirement options).

## Corporate Systems Account Configuration
During your first 48 hours, IT will assist you in configuring your access to our primary business applications:
1. **Corporate Email & Calendar**: Access your mail and schedule through Gmail using your `@guidely.com` address.
2. **Internal Communication**: Join our Slack channels to collaborate with team members across New York, London, and Singapore.
3. **Client CRM Platform**: Operations team members will receive logins for Salesforce to manage active consulting leads.

## Benefits and Perks Enrollment
Guidely provides a premium benefits package to support our employees:
- **Health and Dental Insurance**: Full coverage plans are available for employees and their immediate families.
- **Retirement Planning**: Guidely offers a 401(k) matching program where the company matches up to 4% of your contributions.
- **Wellness Allowance**: Employees are eligible for a monthly reimbursement of $50 USD to cover gym memberships or wellness apps.
