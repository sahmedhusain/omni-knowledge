Guidely New Hire Onboarding

Welcome to Guidely! We are excited to have you on the team.
Your first week plan:
Day 1: Set up your laptop, complete compliance paperwork, and join the team intro call.
Day 2: Configure your development environment following the Developer Setup Guide.
Day 3: Pair program with a buddy to get familiar with our code repositories and branching flow.
Day 4: Pick up your first task from the backlog, create a pull request, and run it by your mentor.
Day 5: Participate in the weekly demo meeting and celebrate your successful first week!
Reach out to your buddy on Slack if you have any questions. Happy onboarding!
